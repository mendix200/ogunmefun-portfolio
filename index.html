01

RETAIL OPERATIONS / WEB APPLICATION

DrinkStore NG

Inventory, sales and profit management for Nigerian drinks retail.

Stock, sales and profit — brought together.

Read full case study
Manage stock → Record sale → Deduct quantity → Review results

The business problem

A drinks shop needs consistent stock records, controlled pricing and a clear view of sales, costs and expenses. Manual tracking makes reconciliation harder and can leave staff responsibilities unclear.

My contribution

Defined and refined requirements through AI-assisted development, including administrator controls, sales quantities, imports, bulk discounts and mobile usability. Requested security reviews and followed up on reported defects.

Scope & implementation history

  • Product and stock management with cost and selling prices.
  • Sales recording and automatic stock deduction, with insufficient-stock warnings.
  • Profit/loss reporting and expense tracking.
  • Administrator and staff permissions, with restricted financial information.
  • Excel/CSV product import, searchable catalogue and configurable discounts.
  • Responsive interface and deployment through AppDeploy.

Decisions: Keep price controls with administrators; prevent requested sale quantities from being silently reduced to available stock; simplify repeated work with product imports and multi-product discount controls.

Evidence: Development records describe deployed iterations and security fixes, including server-side role checks, replay protections, rate limits and authenticator verification for sensitive staff actions. These records are not an independent security certification.

Known follow-up: The retrieved history leaves fully atomic sales transactions and leaked-password protection unresolved. Current production behaviour has not been re-tested for this portfolio. No revenue, user-growth or efficiency metrics are claimed.

02

AUTOMOTIVE SERVICES / WORKSHOP MANAGEMENT

MechanicSolution

Digital workshop records and customer follow-up for Nigerian auto mechanics.

A clearer picture of every service.

Read full case study
Register vehicle → Record service → Issue invoice → Schedule follow-up

The business problem

Workshops that rely on paper records, memory and phone calls can lose customer information and miss service follow-ups. Vehicle owners also need a clearer way to review recorded maintenance history.

My contribution

Built and refined the project through AI-assisted website and app creation, using prompts and iterative requests to develop workshop workflows. Requested vulnerability reviews and follow-up fixes as the application evolved.

Technical foundation

The retrieved source uses React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS, with backend routes and scheduled jobs. Deployment history records the application on AppDeploy.

Features supported by project records

  • Client and vehicle management with service and repair histories.
  • Appointment management and customer-facing workshop pages.
  • Invoices, email delivery and scheduled maintenance reminders.
  • VIN/chassis-based service-history lookup and inspection records.
  • Business analytics, workshop marketplace and an AI service advisor.
  • Authentication, mechanic-scoped records and security-review iterations.

Decisions: Connect clients, vehicles and service records so workshops can manage follow-up from a consistent history. Keep customer-facing journeys distinct from workshop management, and support recurring work with scheduled reminders.

Evidence: Local source files and project conversations document the features and deployed iterations. Reviews addressed access controls, input handling and VIN-lookup rate limits. This portfolio does not constitute a fresh production test or security certification.

Learning: Workshop-submitted VIN records are not independently verified vehicle history. Record provenance and conflicting vehicle details need clear presentation. Customer retention and operational efficiency are intended benefits; no measured impact is claimed.
